# Sample scans

`sample_chest_ct.zip` is case Lung_Dx-A0001 (one 64-slice, 5 mm chest CT
series) from the Lung-PET-CT-Dx collection of The Cancer Imaging Archive. The
collection is public, de-identified to DICOM PS 3.15 Annex E and licensed under
CC BY 4.0.

Li P, Wang S, Li T, et al. A Large-Scale CT and PET/CT Dataset for Lung Cancer
Diagnosis (Lung-PET-CT-Dx). The Cancer Imaging Archive, 2020.
https://doi.org/10.7937/TCIA.2020.NNC2-0461

In public demo mode (`VRSEG_PUBLIC_DEMO=1`) the app processes this scan at
start-up and shows it to every visitor as `Sample_chest_CT`.
